Across the four varieties on the estate, Lara parcels carry the most soil-pathogen pressure, especially Phytophthora root rot. Sara, Chandler and Howard sit within the range we'd expect for walnut in this region. The likely contributors on Lara are lower soil organic carbon and low microbial biodiversity, both of which the platform can track year-on-year. Nothing here calls for immediate intervention — the most useful next step is denser sampling on Lara to confirm where the hot-spots sit before any change in management.

What we want SLM to shape

1
Pathogen unit of aggregation. Named taxa, functional guilds, or both? IC-grade vs operator-grade may need different units.
2
Airborne integration. Assay form (spore trap, qPCR, eDNA from air), cadence, and how it folds into the asset index — single combined number, or separate soil + airborne indices.
3
Audience layering. Two presentation layers (IC, operator) — is that the right split, or do LP / certifier / regulator views need their own render?
4
Pre-acquisition baseline scope. Val del Pino: minimum viable scan, what locks into the protocol, what stays flexible. Sets the template for the 2027 fund.
5
Evidence-package schema. IC, LP, Verra, certifier — what fields, what cadence, what level of provenance exposed. Defines the EvidenceSet shape, not just the report layout.
6
Recommendation intensity. Falsifiable hypotheses (as in this mock) vs prescriptive actions. Joao influences operators; the right intensity is the one that lands without bypassing them.
7
Sampling and monitoring cadence. Per-asset baseline + annual round + event-driven re-sampling — does this shape match how SLM decides interventions, or is the rhythm different?
8
Commercial shape. Per-asset baseline price + monitoring annuity + per-evidence-package fee — does this fit how SLM books and approves work, fund by fund?

Reading guide for the room

Nothing in this mock is a commitment. Numbers, taxa, parcels, and labels are placeholders to make the shape legible. The asks are the questions on this page. The aim is to walk out with a sharper version of the IC view, the operator view, and the fund rollup — co-designed, not pitched.
