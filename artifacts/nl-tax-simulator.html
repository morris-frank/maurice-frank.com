Deliberate simplifications. Employer premies are a single slider, not itemised Awf/Aof/Whk. Pension accrual, holiday allowance, WW/WIA value and sick pay are not monetised on the employment side — they are real and favour employment beyond what is shown. A controlling DGA is assumed not insured for employee insurances, so only the Zvw employer levy applies. Full distribution from the BV is assumed unless the retain toggle is set. The lucratief-belang-via-holding rows assume the doorstootregeling conditions (≥95% distributed in the same calendar year) are met.

Not modelled, and possibly decisive. The risk of total loss on the share purchase — the single largest unmodelled factor on this page. The 30%/27% expat ruling (not applicable here). The Dutch start-up/scale-up option regime (65% inclusion, ~32% effective, targeted 1 Jan 2027, group companies excluded). The box 2 multiplier on doorstoot lucratief belang, deferred to 1 Jan 2028, which would push the holding route toward 36%. German withholding at source and DE–NL treaty apportionment by workdays. Any of these can move the answer by six figures.
